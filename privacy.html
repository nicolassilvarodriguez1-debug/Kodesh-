Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Yajol Investment Group LLC ("we," "our," or "KODESH") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use the KODESH platform (kodeshbible.com), including our web and mobile applications (collectively, the "Service").

By using KODESH, you accept the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Name (if you provide it)
  • Profile photo (optional, if you choose to upload one)
  • Authentication information (through Supabase Auth, including Google sign-in if you use it)

1.2 User-generated content

  • Bookmarks and study notes you save on chapters and verses
  • Your Bible reading progress
  • Your study goals and settings preferences
  • Conversations with the AI Study Assistant and search queries

1.3 Payment information

If you subscribe to KODESH Premium, payment processing is handled by Stripe, a third-party provider. We do not store credit card numbers or complete financial information on our servers. Stripe handles this information in accordance with its own privacy policy.

1.4 Technical and usage information

  • IP address, browser type, and device
  • Pages visited and time of use (through Vercel Web Analytics)
  • Usage logs for AI features (searches, assistant queries, lexicon) — used to enforce plan limits and improve the service

2. How we use your information

We use the information collected to:

  • Provide and maintain the Service (sync your progress, bookmarks, and notes across devices)
  • Process your subscription and manage your plan (free or premium)
  • Operate the AI Study Assistant, Smart Search, and the Lexicon, which use artificial intelligence models from Anthropic (Claude) to generate responses
  • Enforce the usage limits that apply to your plan
  • Improve, debug, and optimize the platform
  • Communicate with you about your account or important changes to the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Use of Artificial Intelligence and sharing with third parties

KODESH uses language models from Anthropic (Claude) to power the Study Assistant, Smart Search, and the Hebrew/Greek Lexicon. When you use these features:

  • The text of your query (and the relevant biblical context, such as the chapter you are reading) is sent to the Anthropic API to generate a response
  • We do not send your name, email, or payment information to Anthropic — only the content needed to answer your query
  • Anthropic processes this data in accordance with its own privacy policy and terms of use for API customers, which generally do not use this data to train its models

4. Data storage and security

Your information is stored on Supabase servers, with industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit (HTTPS) and Row Level Security policies that ensure only you can access your personal bookmarks and notes.

Although we implement reasonable security measures, no system is 100% foolproof. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.

5. Data retention

We retain your information while your account is active. If you request deletion of your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, unless the law requires us to retain certain records (for example, billing records).

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request the correction of inaccurate information
  • Request the deletion of your account and associated data
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Object to certain uses of your information

To exercise these rights, contact us at kodeshbible@gmail.com.

7. Minors

KODESH is designed for a general audience, including families. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 without the verifiable consent of a parent or guardian. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, contact us to have it deleted.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and local storage to keep you signed in, remember your preferences (theme, Bible version, etc.), and analyze site usage. See our Cookie Policy for more details.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new version on this page with a revised update date. Continued use of the Service after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
