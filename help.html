KODESH User Guide

Everything you need to know to get the most out of your Hebrew-Messianic Bible study platform.

Read the Bible

Move between books, chapters and verses
1

Choose Old or New Testament

In the side menu (or the ☰ button on mobile) you'll see two tabs: Old and New. Select the testament you want to read.

2

Select a book

Below it you'll find the full list of books with your reading progress (for example, "6/50" means you've read 6 of the 50 chapters in Genesis). Tap any book to open it.

3

Change chapters

Inside a book you can move to the next or previous chapter with the arrows, or use the chapter selector at the top.

Tip: the green ✓ Read button marks the chapter as completed and updates your progress automatically.
4

Change Bible version

In the top bar you can choose among the available versions (such as the World Messianic Bible, WMB) according to your reading preference.

✶

Hebrew and Greek Lexicon

Discover the original meaning of every word
1

Tap any word in the text

While you read, simply tap or click any word in the verse. KODESH automatically identifies the exact Hebrew (Old Testament) or Greek (New Testament) word used in that specific passage.

2

Explore the lexicon panel

A panel opens with the original word, its transliteration, Strong's number, and definition. Depending on the word, you can also see:

⛪

Biblical Meaning

The deep theological meaning of the word in its Messianic context.

📜

Word Origin

The etymology and roots of the term in the original language.

💡

Did you know?

Interesting facts that enrich your understanding of the text.

❤️

Apply it

A practical reflection for your life of faith today.

3

Explore related words and key verses

Tap any related word to open its own analysis, or tap a verse under "Explore in Scripture" to jump straight to that passage.

4

Copy or share

Use the ⎘ and ↗ icons at the top of the panel to copy the analysis to your clipboard or share it directly.

📜

Interlinear

Read the biblical text word by word in its original language
1

Open the Interlinear

Inside any chapter, tap the 📜 Interlinear button next to "Study" and "Notes".

2

Read word by word

Each verse is broken down into its original words (Hebrew or Greek), with the transliteration and translation shown beneath each one. That way you can follow the verse in the original language without losing the meaning.

3

Tap any Strong's number

Above each word you'll see its Strong's code (such as H7225 or G3056). Tap it to open the full lexicon entry for that specific word.

Note: the first time you open a chapter, the interlinear may take a few seconds to generate. After that it loads instantly because KODESH caches it.

Bookmarks and Notes

Highlight, annotate and save your favorite verses
1

Open the verse actions panel

On any verse, use one of these gestures:

📱 Double tap on mobile 🖱️ Right click on a computer

A panel slides up from the bottom with all the actions available for that verse.

2

Choose a quick action

✦

Explain

Opens the Study Assistant with an explanation of the verse.

📜

Interlinear

Jumps straight to the interlinear view of that chapter.

📖

Deep Study

Structured analysis: historical context, original language, Messianic connections and application.

📝

Note

Opens the chapter's notes panel so you can write your reflections.

↗

Share

Shares the verse with its reference included.

⎘

Copy

Copies the verse text to your clipboard.

3

Highlight with a color

Further down in the same panel, choose one of the available colors to mark the verse by what it means to you:

Promise Teaching Messianic Warning Prayer Prophecy General
4

Add an optional note and save

You can write a short personal note before tapping ✓ Save bookmark. To remove an existing bookmark, tap Remove.

5

Review all your bookmarks

Go to the Bookmarks tab in the bottom navigation (mobile) or the side menu (desktop) to see the full list, filter by color, and jump straight to each saved verse.

✦

Study Assistant

Your artificial intelligence companion for going deeper into Scripture
1

Open the assistant

Tap the ✦ Study button in any chapter, or the ✦ icon in the top bar. It also opens automatically when you use "Explain" or "Deep Study" from a verse's panel.

2

Ask any Bible question

You can ask about the meaning of a passage, connections between the Torah and the New Testament, historical context, Messianic fulfillment in Yeshua, or any theological question from a Hebrew-Messianic perspective.

3

Keep the chapter's context

The assistant knows which book and chapter you're reading, so your questions are answered with that context in mind without you having to repeat it.

Tip: the free plan includes a limited number of monthly questions to the assistant. The Premium plan offers expanded use.
🔍

Search

Find verses, words or topics across the whole Bible
1

Open Search

Tap 🔍 Search in the top bar or in the bottom navigation.

2

Type your search

You can search for a keyword, a phrase, or an exact reference (such as "Genesis 1:1" or "John 3:16"). The results show the full verse with the match highlighted.

3

Tap a result

It takes you straight to that chapter and verse in the Bible.

🔥

Study cycle and Reading streak

Follow the reading calendar of the weekly portions (parashot)
1

See the current portion

At the top of each book you'll see a box with the Parashah of the week, its Hebrew name, and the range of chapters it covers — for example, "Bereshit — In the Beginning · Genesis 1:1–6:8".

2

Track your progress

The colored squares next to the portion's name show how many chapters of that parashah you've already marked as read. Tap View Cycle to see the full calendar of all the portions of the year.

3

Keep your streak

The 🔥 icon with a number in the top bar shows how many consecutive days you've read the Bible in KODESH. Read at least one chapter every day to keep your streak alive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about using KODESH
Why does the interlinear take a while to load the first time?
KODESH generates the word-by-word analysis using artificial intelligence the first time anyone opens that chapter. Once generated, it's saved and loads instantly for every user after that.
Why don't some words show the lexicon when I tap them?
Grammatical words such as articles, prepositions or conjunctions (like "the", "of", "and") don't have their own lexical entry in Hebrew or Greek, so no panel appears when you tap them.
Can I use KODESH without an internet connection?
Basic Bible reading works with a limited connection, but the artificial intelligence features (Lexicon, Interlinear, Study Assistant) require an active internet connection.
How do I switch between light and dark mode?
Tap the ☀️/🌙 icon in the app's top bar to switch between the two visual themes.
Are my bookmarks and notes saved in the cloud?
Yes. All your bookmarks, notes and reading progress sync to your account, so you can access them from any device where you sign in.
