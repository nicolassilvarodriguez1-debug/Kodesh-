Delete my KODESH account

Last updated: August 25, 2026

You can delete your KODESH account, from either the mobile app or the web version, at any time. Deletion is permanent and irreversible.

How to delete your account

  1. Sign in to KODESH (mobile app or kodeshbible.com) with your email and password.
  2. Go to your Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete account".
  4. Type DELETE in the confirmation field and tap "Delete account".

You can also do it directly from your browser by going to your profile:

Go to my profile →

If you would prefer that we do it for you, write to us at kodeshbible@gmail.com from the email associated with your account, stating that you are requesting the deletion of your account.

What is deleted

  • Your login account (email, authentication)
  • Your Bible reading progress
  • Your bookmarks and study notes
  • Your reading streak
  • Your usage history for AI features (assistant, search, lexicon)
  • Your associated plan/subscription

What is retained and for how long

If you had a Premium subscription, we may retain the associated billing records (through Stripe) for the period required by legal and accounting obligations, even after your account is deleted. These records are not used for any other purpose.

For more details on how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.
