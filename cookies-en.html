Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Yajol Investment Group LLC ("we") uses cookies and similar storage technologies on KODESH (kodeshbible.com).

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. We also use similar technologies such as local storage (localStorage), which works in a similar way but remains in your browser until it is cleared manually.

2. How we use cookies and local storage

2.1 Essential (necessary for the Service to function)

  • Authentication: keeping you signed in (managed by Supabase Auth)
  • App preferences: light/dark theme, selected Bible version, Interlinear Mode preference

These cannot be disabled without affecting the basic functioning of KODESH.

2.2 Functional

  • Reading progress, bookmarks, and notes: saved locally and synced with your cloud account
  • "Promise of the day" indicators: so we don't show you the same promise more than once a day

2.3 Analytics

  • Vercel Web Analytics: collects aggregated and anonymized data about visits, page views, and devices, to understand how KODESH is used and improve the platform. This tool is designed not to use individually identifying cookies or track users across sites.

3. Third-party cookies

ProviderPurpose

These providers may set their own cookies in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

4. How to control cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Keep in mind that disabling essential cookies may prevent features such as sign-in, bookmarks, and progress syncing from working properly.

Most browsers allow you to:

  • See which cookies you have stored and delete them individually
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block cookies from specific sites
  • Block all cookies
  • Delete all cookies when you close the browser

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the technologies we use. We will post any changes on this page with a revised update date.
